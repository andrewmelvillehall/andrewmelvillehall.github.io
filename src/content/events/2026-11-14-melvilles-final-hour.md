## Ball
Inspired by the Masque of the Red Death, this year's AMH Ball "Melville's final hour" will feature 6 coloured themes throughout the
night with a dramatic act at midnight...  The night will feature numerous music performances and plenty of ceilidhs with breaks for
awards and celebration in between. It is a night you can't miss.

Drinks, food, and ice cream will be served throughout the night to keep everyone dancing.

The dress code will be formal with an emphasis on colourful outfits and extravagance.

### Timings
Doors open 7pm on Saturday the 14th of November and the festivities will go on till 1am in Younger Hall.

### Tickets
Tickets will be sold by reception after Halloween, 20£ for residents and 25£ for guests.
