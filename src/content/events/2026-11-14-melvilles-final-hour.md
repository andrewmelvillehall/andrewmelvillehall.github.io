## Welcome Letter
# Dear Melvillites and Honoured Guests, 

To celebrate our time together in Melville so far, we are overjoyed to invite you to this year's Andrew Melville Hall Ball;
 **Melville's Final Hour**. 

It will be a night of opulence and excess, with dances and performances throughout the night, including some by our very own. The night will feature six coloured themes marked by the hour. Come adorned in formal yet danceable attire, in a palette of your choice, whether dark and mysterious or in your finest colours.

Join us in recognising those who have rocked the boat a little harder than usual this year with awards and prizes, and indulge in our on-site bar, reception drinks, cakes, and ice cream. As is with every night of indulgence, masques will be optional but dress must be grand.

Please be aware, dear guests, as we find ourselves in turbulent times. With regret, we can now confirm recent speculations surrounding a ghost haunting the (carpeted) hallways of Melville, and encourage all Melvillites and their guests to remain vigilant.

Therefore, we’re so very glad to bring you within the safety of Younger Hall's fortified walls on the evening of the 14th of November, from 7:00PM onwards.

## Ball
Inspired by the Masque of the Red Death, this year's AMH Ball "Melville's final hour" will feature 6 coloured themes throughout the
night with a dramatic act at midnight...  The night will feature numerous music performances and plenty of ceilidhs with breaks for
awards and celebration in between. It is a night you can't miss.

Drinks, food, and ice cream will be served throughout the night to keep everyone dancing.

The dress code will be formal with an emphasis on colourful outfits and extravagance.

### Timings
Doors open 7pm on Saturday the 14th of November and the festivities will go on till 1am in Younger Hall.

### Tickets
Tickets will be sold by reception after Halloween, 20£ for residents and 25£ for guests.

We await you...
